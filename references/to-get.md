## Dretske, *Knowledge and the Flow of Information*

Fred I. Dretske, 1981 (MIT Press, Bradford Books; Blackwell in the UK).
Reissued by CSLI Publications, 1999, The David Hume Series, 273 pp. Wanted
for the "loss is meaning" idea: the analog-to-digital step from perception
to concept (chapter to confirm in the book).

Options, checked 2026-10-04:

- **Ebook**, ISBN 978-1575867151, $23 from the University of Chicago Press
  (distributor). Adobe-protected PDF (`.acsm`, opens in Adobe Digital
  Editions with an Adobe ID). Claude cannot read the file itself, but can
  read it on screen in Adobe Digital Editions via computer use.
- **Paperback**, ISBN 978-1575861951, $30 list from the University of
  Chicago Press; in print but 0 on hand there, and out of stock on Amazon.
  AbeBooks: new and used from about $35.
- **Borrow**: Internet Archive lending copy (archive.org/details/knowledgeflowofi0000dret).

Listing: https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/distributed/K/bo3642299.html
